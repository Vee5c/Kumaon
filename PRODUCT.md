## Platform

web

## Stack
Delegated by the user, with the constraint "open source, good for animations and media file integration". Chosen: Astro (static output, optimized image/video handling) with Motion and Lenis for animation and smooth scroll, all MIT-licensed. GSAP is free to use but not OSI open source, so it is optional, not a default. Currently a single static `index.html` with local `images/` and `videos/`.

## Users
Mixed audience, no single primary: small and local brands (restaurants, startups) wanting ads and reels; marketing teams and agencies hiring a production partner; musicians, podcasters and creators wanting music videos and podcast shoots. Visitors evaluate the work first, then reach out.

## Product Purpose
Kumaon & Co. is an independent video production house in Bangalore (since 2025) making ads, music videos, podcasts and social content, shot, scored and cut in-house. The landing page exists to show the work and turn visits into WhatsApp conversations.

## Positioning
Built on music and sound design: "an ear before an eye". Cuts start from how they sound and the picture follows. Work is shaped around each client's brand, not a reused house style. Founder-led: founded and shot by Vee.

## Operating Context
Clients reach the studio over WhatsApp (preferred) or Instagram. Full-length pieces live on Google Drive; the site shows short clips with posters.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services shown: brand ads, fast-turnaround edits, social reels, podcasts, music videos.

## Brand Commitments
Binding, per the user: the tagline "You dream, we deliver." and the existing copy, the WhatsApp-first CTA, the KUMAON & CO. name and logo mark (`images/logo-mark.png`, `images/logo-mark-large.png`), and the founder's presence ("Founded & shot by Vee", `images/vee-founder.jpg`).

## Evidence on Hand
Five video clips with posters in `videos/` and `images/`: Sato holiday campaign (brand ad), Lyfe pizza cut and Lyfe feed reel, a podcast shoot, and a metal music video. Full cuts on Google Drive. Absent: client testimonials, logos of past clients beyond Sato and Lyfe, metrics, awards, press. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. Work first: the footage is the proof, and every section serves getting it seen.
2. Sound is the differentiator; audio and rhythm should be felt in how the page behaves.
3. One low-friction next step: message on WhatsApp.
4. Show range without implying a house style; each piece reads as built for its client.
5. Keep claims to what is true and evidenced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Not specified by the user. Defaults to honoring reduced-motion preferences and keeping video controls and captions-ready markup usable.
